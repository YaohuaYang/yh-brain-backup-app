Privacy Policy for YH-Brain Backup

This app has one user, its owner. It can only see and change the files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive (Google scope drive.file). It writes encrypted backup data there and reads it back to check that the data is intact. It does not collect, share or sell any data, and nothing is sent anywhere other than the owner's own Google Drive.
